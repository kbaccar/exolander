KOI-351 f

KOI-351 f is a hot water-rich world orbiting a bright white-yellow star 2,767 ly away. A year there lasts 124.9 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
