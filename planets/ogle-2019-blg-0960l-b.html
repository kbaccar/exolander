OGLE-2019-BLG-0960L b

OGLE-2019-BLG-0960L b is a frozen super-Earth orbiting a star 3,196 ly away. It was found using gravitational microlensing: its gravity briefly magnified a more distant star. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of OGLE-2019-BLG-0960L b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: None, frozen out. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 91 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 39 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
