L 98-59 d

L 98-59 d is a hot water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 34.6 ly away. A year there lasts 7.5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
