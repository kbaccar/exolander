Kepler-372 c

Kepler-372 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 4,873 ly away. A year there lasts 20.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-372 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.
