TOI-5624 f

TOI-5624 f is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 332.4 ly away. A year there lasts 45.4 days. It was found using tiny timing shifts in a neighboring planet's transits. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-5624 f. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on bare, cratered rock with no air.

  • Air: None (bare rock). Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 68 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 52 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.
