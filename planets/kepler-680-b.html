Kepler-680 b

Kepler-680 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 4,868 ly away. A year there lasts 3.7 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-680 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.004 bar.
