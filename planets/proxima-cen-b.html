Proxima Cen b

The closest known exoplanet, just 4.2 light-years away. It sits in its red dwarf's habitable zone, but the star blasts it with frequent flares and it is probably tidally locked. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.
