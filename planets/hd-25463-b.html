HD 25463 b

HD 25463 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 148.8 ly away. A year there lasts 7.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
