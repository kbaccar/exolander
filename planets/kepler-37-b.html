Kepler-37 b

Kepler-37 b is a hot small rocky planet orbiting a Sun-like star 208.5 ly away. A year there lasts 13.4 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-37 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.49 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 7 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 481 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.
