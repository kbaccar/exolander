Kepler-347 c

Kepler-347 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 4,236 ly away. A year there lasts 27.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
