TOI-1472 c

TOI-1472 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 397.4 ly away. A year there lasts 15.5 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-1472 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.66 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 133 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 26 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.
