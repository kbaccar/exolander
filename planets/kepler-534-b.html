Kepler-534 b

Kepler-534 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 1,861 ly away. A year there lasts 16 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-534 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.55 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 79 kg.
