HR 858 d

HR 858 d is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 104.3 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 11.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of HR 858 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.
