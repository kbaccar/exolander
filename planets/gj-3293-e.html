GJ 3293 e

GJ 3293 e is a warm water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 65.9 ly away. A year there lasts 13.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 3293 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 5.7 bar.

  • The air is about 6 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
