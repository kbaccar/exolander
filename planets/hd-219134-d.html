HD 219134 d

HD 219134 d is a hot water-rich world orbiting an orange star 21.3 ly away. A year there lasts 46.9 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
