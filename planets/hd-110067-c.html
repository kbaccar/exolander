HD 110067 c

HD 110067 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 104.9 ly away. Its star system has 3 stars. A year there lasts 13.7 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
