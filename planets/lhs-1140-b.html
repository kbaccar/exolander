LHS 1140 b

A dense super-Earth 49 light-years away. JWST hints at a nitrogen atmosphere, and it may be an ice-covered world with a liquid ocean under the side facing its star. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of LHS 1140 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen) (JWST hint, guess). Surface pressure: 1 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 131 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 27 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
