Kepler-1261 b

Kepler-1261 b is a warm water-rich world orbiting an orange star 2,428 ly away. A year there lasts 48.4 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-1261 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 9.9 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 79 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 45 cm.
  • The air is about 10 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
