Kepler-1921 c

Kepler-1921 c is a scorching super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 3,030 ly away. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
