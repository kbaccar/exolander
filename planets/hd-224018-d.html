HD 224018 d

HD 224018 d is a hot water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 344 ly away. A year there lasts 138.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of HD 224018 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 10.6 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 51 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 69 cm.
  • The air is about 11 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
