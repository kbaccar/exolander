Kepler-1874 b

Kepler-1874 b is a warm water-rich world orbiting an orange star 1,331 ly away. A year there lasts 56.6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
