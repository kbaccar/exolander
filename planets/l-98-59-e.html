L 98-59 e

L 98-59 e is a warm super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 34.6 ly away. A year there lasts 12.8 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of L 98-59 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.
