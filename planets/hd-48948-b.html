HD 48948 b

HD 48948 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 55.1 ly away. A year there lasts 7.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
