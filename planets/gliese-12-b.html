Gliese 12 b

A temperate world between Earth and Venus in size and warmth, 40 light-years away. It is a key target for testing whether such planets keep their atmospheres.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Gliese 12 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.7 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 77 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 46 cm.
