Kepler-445 d

Kepler-445 d is a temperate super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 414.9 ly away. A year there lasts 8.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Note: its mass is estimated from its size. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.
