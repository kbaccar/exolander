Kepler-439 b

Kepler-439 b is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 2,469 ly away. A year there lasts 178.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-439 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 8.6 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 79 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 44 cm.
  • The air is about 9 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
