Kepler-402 b

Kepler-402 b is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a bright white-yellow star 2,040 ly away. A year there lasts 4 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-402 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on glowing iron-rich lava fields.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 93 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 38 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.
