GJ 887 c

GJ 887 c is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 10.7 ly away. A year there lasts 21.8 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
