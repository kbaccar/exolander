Titan

Moon of Saturn · 9.5× Earth's distance from the Sun

Saturn's largest moon, and the only moon with a thick atmosphere. It has rain, rivers, lakes and seas, but they are liquid methane and ethane at −179 °C.

Walking on Titan

  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 10 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 363 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.

Landmarks in the game

  • Methane lake: Titan has lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane, the only other known world with stable surface liquid.
  • Dune field: Titan’s equatorial dunes are built from hydrocarbon grains and stretch for hundreds of kilometers.

Real maps, real physics. Gravity, temperature and air pressure are the real values, and they change how you move, what you hear and how long your suit lasts.
