Kepler-105 c

Kepler-105 c is a hot super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 1,498 ly away. A year there lasts 7.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-105 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.005 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 189 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 19 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.
