EPIC 212624936 b

EPIC 212624936 b is a frozen Earth-size planet orbiting a Sun-like star 1,833 ly away. A year there lasts 14 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
