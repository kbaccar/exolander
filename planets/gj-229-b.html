GJ 229 b

GJ 229 b is a frozen water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 18.8 ly away. Its star system has 3 stars. A year there lasts 579.5 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
