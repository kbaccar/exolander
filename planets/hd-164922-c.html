HD 164922 c

HD 164922 c is a warm water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 71.8 ly away. A year there lasts 75.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
