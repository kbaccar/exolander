GJ 667 C e

GJ 667 C e is a cold super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 23.6 ly away. Its star system has 3 stars. A year there lasts 62.2 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.
