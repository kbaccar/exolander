K2-317 b

K2-317 b is a hot water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 575 ly away. A year there lasts 6.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of K2-317 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 7.2 bar.

  • The air is about 7 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
