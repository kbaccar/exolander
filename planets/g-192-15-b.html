G 192-15 b

G 192-15 b is a hot Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 31.1 ly away. A year there lasts 2.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
