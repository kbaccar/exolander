HD 15337 b

HD 15337 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 146.2 ly away. A year there lasts 4.8 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
