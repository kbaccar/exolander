Kepler-411 d

Kepler-411 d is a hot water-rich world orbiting an orange star 501.2 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 58 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-411 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 6.8 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 97 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 36 cm.
  • The air is about 7 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
