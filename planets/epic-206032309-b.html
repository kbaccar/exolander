EPIC 206032309 b

EPIC 206032309 b is a frozen Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 526.3 ly away. A year there lasts 2.9 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
