BD+05 4868 A b

BD+05 4868 A b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 142.1 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 1.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass.
