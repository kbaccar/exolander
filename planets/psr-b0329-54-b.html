PSR B0329+54 b

PSR B0329+54 b is a frozen Earth-size planet orbiting a pulsar (the dead core of an exploded star) 1,647 ly away. A year there lasts 27.8 years. It was found using tiny changes in its pulsar's radio pulses. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
