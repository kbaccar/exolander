TOI-500 c

TOI-500 c is a frozen water-rich world orbiting an orange star 154.6 ly away. A year there lasts 6.6 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-500 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: thin N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.08 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 81 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 43 cm.
