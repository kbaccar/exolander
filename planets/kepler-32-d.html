Kepler-32 d

Kepler-32 d is a temperate water-rich world orbiting an orange star 1,056 ly away. A year there lasts 22.8 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-32 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep ocean under a hydrogen sky.

  • Air: Rich in H₂ (hydrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 28 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 74 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 47 cm.
  • The air is about 28 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
