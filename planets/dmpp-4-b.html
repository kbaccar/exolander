DMPP-4 b

DMPP-4 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 82.6 ly away. A year there lasts 3.5 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of DMPP-4 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 69 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 51 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.
