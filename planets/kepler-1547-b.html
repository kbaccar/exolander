Kepler-1547 b

Kepler-1547 b is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a bright white-yellow star 2,201 ly away. A year there lasts 17 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
