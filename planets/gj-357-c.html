GJ 357 c

GJ 357 c is a hot water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 30.8 ly away. A year there lasts 9.1 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
