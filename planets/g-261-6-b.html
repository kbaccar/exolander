G 261-6 b

G 261-6 b is a temperate Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 34.7 ly away. A year there lasts 5.5 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
