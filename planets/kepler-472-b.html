Kepler-472 b

Kepler-472 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 1,000 ly away. A year there lasts 4.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-472 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.004 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 71 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 49 cm.
