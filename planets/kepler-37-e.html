Kepler-37 e

Kepler-37 e is a hot small rocky planet orbiting an orange star 208.5 ly away. A year there lasts 51.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-37 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on rocky land with shallow seas.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), CO₂ (carbon dioxide), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.53 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 15 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 228 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.
