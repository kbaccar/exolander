HR 858 c

HR 858 c is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 104.3 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
