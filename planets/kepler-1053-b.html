Kepler-1053 b

Kepler-1053 b is a hot Earth-size planet orbiting an orange star 492.9 ly away. A year there lasts 2.4 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 66 kg.
