TOI-1235 b

TOI-1235 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 129.3 ly away. A year there lasts 3.4 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-1235 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.88 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 164 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 21 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
