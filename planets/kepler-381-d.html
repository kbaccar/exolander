Kepler-381 d

Kepler-381 d is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a bright white-yellow star 871.9 ly away. A year there lasts 8.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
