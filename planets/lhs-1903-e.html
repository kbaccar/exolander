LHS 1903 e

LHS 1903 e is a warm water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 116.4 ly away. A year there lasts 29.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of LHS 1903 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 6.7 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 135 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 26 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.
  • The air is about 7 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
