G 264-012 c

G 264-012 c is a warm water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 52.2 ly away. A year there lasts 8.1 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
