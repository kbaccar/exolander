TOI-5800 b

TOI-5800 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 139.9 ly away. A year there lasts 2.6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
