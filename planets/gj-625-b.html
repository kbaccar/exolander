GJ 625 b

GJ 625 b is a warm super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 21.1 ly away. A year there lasts 14.6 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
