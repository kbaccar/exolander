DMPP-6 b

DMPP-6 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a bright white-yellow star 178.2 ly away. A year there lasts 7.6 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
