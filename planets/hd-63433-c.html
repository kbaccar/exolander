HD 63433 c

HD 63433 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 73.1 ly away. A year there lasts 20.5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of HD 63433 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.39 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 150 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 23 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.
