GJ 1289 b

GJ 1289 b is a cold water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 27.2 ly away. A year there lasts 111.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 1289 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 8.9 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 77 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 45 cm.
  • The air is about 9 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
