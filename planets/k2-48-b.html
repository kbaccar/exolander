K2-48 b

K2-48 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 1,023 ly away. A year there lasts 20.5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
