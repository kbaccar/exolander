GJ 411 b

GJ 411 b is a warm super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 18.5 ly away. A year there lasts 12.9 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 411 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 6.5 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 90 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 39 cm.
  • The air is about 6 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
