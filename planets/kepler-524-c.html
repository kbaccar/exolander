Kepler-524 c

Kepler-524 c is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a bright white-yellow star 3,452 ly away. A year there lasts 1.9 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
