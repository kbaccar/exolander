GJ 536 c

GJ 536 c is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 34 ly away. A year there lasts 32.8 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 536 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 7.8 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 78 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 45 cm.
  • The air is about 8 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
