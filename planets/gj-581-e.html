GJ 581 e

GJ 581 e is a hot super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 20.5 ly away. A year there lasts 3.2 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 581 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on bare, cratered rock with no air.

  • Air: None (bare rock). Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 91 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 38 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
