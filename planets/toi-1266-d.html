TOI-1266 d

TOI-1266 d is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 117.5 ly away. A year there lasts 32.5 days. It was found using tiny timing shifts in a neighboring planet's transits. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-1266 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.
