K2-100 b

K2-100 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 616.3 ly away. A year there lasts 1.7 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of K2-100 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on glowing iron-rich lava fields.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 101 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 35 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
