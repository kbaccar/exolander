K2-201 b

K2-201 b is a frozen super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 645.6 ly away. A year there lasts 1.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
