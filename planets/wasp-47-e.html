WASP-47 e

WASP-47 e is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 863.6 ly away. A year there lasts 19 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
