HD 136352 d

HD 136352 d is a hot water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 47.9 ly away. A year there lasts 107.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
