Kepler-879 c

Kepler-879 c is a frozen small rocky planet orbiting a Sun-like star 2,618 ly away. A year there lasts 16 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-879 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: thin N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.14 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 17 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 200 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
