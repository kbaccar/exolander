Kepler-100 d

Kepler-100 d is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 993.6 ly away. A year there lasts 35.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-100 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.9 bar.

  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 43 cm.
