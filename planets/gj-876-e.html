GJ 876 e

GJ 876 e is a cold water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 15.2 ly away. A year there lasts 124.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of GJ 876 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep ocean under a hydrogen sky.

  • Air: Rich in H₂ (hydrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 28 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 67 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 53 cm.
  • The air is about 28 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
