Kepler-1010 b

Kepler-1010 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting an orange star 1,889 ly away. A year there lasts 34.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-1010 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on dry, dusty desert rock.

  • Air: thin CO₂ (carbon dioxide), N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.21 bar.

  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 45 cm.
