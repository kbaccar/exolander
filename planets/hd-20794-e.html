HD 20794 e

HD 20794 e is a warm water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 19.6 ly away. A year there lasts 147 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of HD 20794 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.

  • Air: H₂O (water vapor) steam, N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 6 bar.
