TOI-1117 d

TOI-1117 d is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 546.6 ly away. A year there lasts 8.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-1117 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 70 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 50 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
