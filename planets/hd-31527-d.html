HD 31527 d

HD 31527 d is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 125.4 ly away. A year there lasts 271.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of HD 31527 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep ocean under a hydrogen sky.

  • Air: Rich in H₂ (hydrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 28 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 69 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 51 cm.
  • The air is about 28 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.
