Kepler-101 c

Kepler-101 c is a scorching super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 3,027 ly away. A year there lasts 6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-101 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on sulfur volcanoes and yellow lava.

  • Air: SO₂ (sulfur dioxide), volcanic · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.005 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 169 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 21 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
