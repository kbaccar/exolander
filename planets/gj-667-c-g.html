GJ 667 C g

GJ 667 C g is a frozen water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 23.6 ly away. Its star system has 3 stars. A year there lasts 256.2 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
