TOI-1442 b

TOI-1442 b is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 134.3 ly away. A year there lasts 10 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-1442 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on bare, cratered rock with no air.

  • Air: None (bare rock). Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 87 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 40 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.
