KOI-55 c

KOI-55 c is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a hot blue-white star 4,015 ly away. A year there lasts 8 hours. It was found using the brightness changes it causes as it orbits.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of KOI-55 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 61 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 57 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
