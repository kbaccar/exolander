Gl 725 B c

Gl 725 B c is a cold water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 22.3 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 37.9 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

Nobody has seen the surface of Gl 725 B c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.
