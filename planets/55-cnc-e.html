55 Cnc e

A super-Earth so close to its star that its year lasts 18 hours and its surface likely holds a magma ocean. JWST found signs of an atmosphere, possibly carbon monoxide or dioxide.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of 55 Cnc e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: possibly CO (carbon monoxide) / CO₂ (carbon dioxide) (JWST hint). Surface pressure: 1 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 159 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 22 cm.
  • Every step is hard work: your legs carry far more than your body weight.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
