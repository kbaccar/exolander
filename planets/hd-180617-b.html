HD 180617 b

HD 180617 b is a cold water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 19.3 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 105.9 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
