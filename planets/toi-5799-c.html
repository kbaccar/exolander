TOI-5799 c

TOI-5799 c is a warm water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 90.7 ly away. A year there lasts 14 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
