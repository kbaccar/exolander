GJ 3998 b

GJ 3998 b is a hot super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 59.2 ly away. A year there lasts 2.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
