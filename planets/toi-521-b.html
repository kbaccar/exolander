TOI-521 b

TOI-521 b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 198.8 ly away. A year there lasts 1.5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
