EPIC 206042996 c

EPIC 206042996 c is a frozen Earth-size planet orbiting an orange star 1,474 ly away. A year there lasts 8 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
