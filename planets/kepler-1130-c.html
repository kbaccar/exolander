Kepler-1130 c

Kepler-1130 c is a scorching small rocky planet orbiting a Sun-like star 813.2 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 3.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
