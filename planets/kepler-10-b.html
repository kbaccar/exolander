What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-10 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.004 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 105 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 33 cm.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
