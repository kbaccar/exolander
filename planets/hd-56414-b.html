HD 56414 b

HD 56414 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a hot blue-white star 887.9 ly away. A year there lasts 29.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
