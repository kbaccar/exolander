HD 158259 b

HD 158259 b is a scorching super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 88.2 ly away. A year there lasts 2.2 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
