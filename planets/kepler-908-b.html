Kepler-908 b

Kepler-908 b is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a Sun-like star 948.6 ly away. Its star system has 3 stars. A year there lasts 1.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
