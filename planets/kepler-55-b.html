Kepler-55 b

Kepler-55 b is a warm water-rich world orbiting an orange star 1,888 ly away. A year there lasts 28 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-55 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on hot, steamy shores of a water world.
