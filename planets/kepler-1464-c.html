Kepler-1464 c

Kepler-1464 c is a scorching Earth-size planet orbiting a Sun-like star 3,756 ly away. A year there lasts 5.3 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
