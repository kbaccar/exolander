LHS 1903 c

LHS 1903 c is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 116.4 ly away. A year there lasts 6.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
