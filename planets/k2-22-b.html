K2-22 b

K2-22 b is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 795.3 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 9 hours. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
