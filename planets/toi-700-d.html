TOI-700 d

The first Earth-size planet in a habitable zone found by NASA's TESS mission (2020). It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of TOI-700 d. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on rocky land with shallow seas.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), CO₂ (carbon dioxide), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 1.41 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 84 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 41 cm.
