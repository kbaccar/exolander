Kepler-560 b

Kepler-560 b is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 356.5 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 18.5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.
