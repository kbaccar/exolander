YZ Cet d

YZ Cet d is a warm Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 12.1 ly away. A year there lasts 4.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
