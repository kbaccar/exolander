K2-227 b

K2-227 b is a frozen water-rich world orbiting a Sun-like star 610.7 ly away. A year there lasts 13.6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.
