TOI-1772.01

TOI-1772.01 is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 318.8 ly away. A year there lasts 8.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
