MOA-2010-BLG-328L b

MOA-2010-BLG-328L b is a frozen water-rich world orbiting a star 2,642 ly away. It was found using gravitational microlensing: its gravity briefly magnified a more distant star. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of MOA-2010-BLG-328L b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: None, frozen out. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 72 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 49 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
