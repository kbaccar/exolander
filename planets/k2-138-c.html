K2-138 c

K2-138 c is a scorching large super-Earth orbiting a Sun-like star 660.7 ly away. A year there lasts 3.6 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of K2-138 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a molten surface of lava seas.

  • Air: rock vapor · a guess. Surface pressure: almost none.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 83 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 42 cm.
  • With almost no air, a hammer and a feather fall side by side, and sound cannot travel.
