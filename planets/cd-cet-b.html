CD Cet b

CD Cet b is a hot large super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 28.1 ly away. A year there lasts 2.3 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known.
