PSR B1257+12 b

PSR B1257+12 b is a frozen small rocky planet orbiting a pulsar (the dead core of an exploded star) 1,957 ly away. A year there lasts 25.3 days. It was found using tiny changes in its pulsar's radio pulses. Note: its temperature is unknown, so the game assumes one.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of PSR B1257+12 b. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a frozen shell of ice.

  • Air: thin N₂ (nitrogen) · a guess. Surface pressure: 0.1 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 12 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 289 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
