TOI-1450 A c

TOI-1450 A c is a hot Earth-size planet orbiting a red dwarf star 73.2 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 5.1 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: only a minimum mass is known.
