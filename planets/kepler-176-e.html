Kepler-176 e

Kepler-176 e is a warm super-Earth orbiting an orange star 1,720 ly away. A year there lasts 51.2 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front.

What it would be like in Worldwalk imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Kepler-176 e. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep global ocean with rocky islands.

  • Air: N₂ (nitrogen), H₂O (water vapor) · a guess. Surface pressure: 7.2 bar.
  • A 70 kg explorer would feel like 17 kg.
  • A jump that clears 50 cm on Earth would reach about 202 cm.
  • Each jump floats for seconds before you drift back down.
  • The air is about 7 times denser than at sea level on Earth: thick enough to slow a falling rock.

Pack the right suit. Choose 3 of 6 modules (cooling, heating, pressure hull, extra oxygen, radiation shield, jetpack), complete 3 surveys and get back to the lander before your oxygen runs out.
