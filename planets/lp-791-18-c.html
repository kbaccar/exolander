LP 791-18 c

LP 791-18 c is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 86.4 ly away. A year there lasts 5 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on.
