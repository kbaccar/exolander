Kepler-1319 b

Kepler-1319 b is a hot super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 338.9 ly away. Its star system has 2 stars. A year there lasts 2.9 days. It was found using the transit method: it dims its star as it passes in front. Note: its mass is estimated from its size.
