Wolf 1061 c

Wolf 1061 c is a temperate water-rich world orbiting a red dwarf star 14 ly away. A year there lasts 17.9 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Planets this size may be wrapped in thick gas; the game imagines it as a solid or water world you can walk on. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.

What it would be like in Exolander imagined

Nobody has seen the surface of Wolf 1061 c. The game builds it from the real numbers above: you land on a deep ocean under a hydrogen sky.
