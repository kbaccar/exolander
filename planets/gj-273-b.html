GJ 273 b

GJ 273 b is a temperate super-Earth orbiting a red dwarf star 19.3 ly away. A year there lasts 18.7 days. It was found using the radial velocity method: it makes its star wobble. Its temperature could allow liquid water, which makes it a habitable-zone candidate, not a confirmed habitable world. Note: its size is estimated from its mass, only a minimum mass is known. It sits where a planet could be warm enough for liquid water, in the so-called habitable zone.
