Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Exolander website (https://exolander.space), the game played on it, and the Exolander apps for Android and iPhone. They are published by Karim Baccar, Luxembourg ("we"). Questions: karim.baccar9@hotmail.com.

What we collect

You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress and settings are stored only in your browser (local storage) or on your device, and never sent to us. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

On the website, ads are provided by Google AdSense (including H5 Games Ads). In the apps, ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google LLC uses cookies, your IP address, device identifiers and information about your visit or app use to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised only if you agree.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before ads are personalised, a consent message from Google asks for your choice. On the website you can change it with the "Privacy and cookie settings" link Google shows at the bottom of the page; in the apps, use Privacy settings in the main menu.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you only watch one when you choose to, in exchange for an in-game benefit.
  • iPhone: the app asks before using your advertising identifier. Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads. In your browser you can block or delete cookies.

Purchases (Explorer Pass)

On the website, the Explorer Pass is sold by Gumroad, Inc., which processes the payment as the seller of record. Gumroad shares with us your email address, the order and your license key so we can support you; we don't receive your card details. When you enter your key in the game, your browser sends it to Gumroad to check it, and the key is saved in your browser so the pass stays unlocked. See the Gumroad Privacy Policy. In the apps, purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store; we only learn that the pass was bought.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Legal bases and your rights

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the game; purchase data is needed to perform the contract. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold (in practice, purchase emails), object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Luxembourg, the CNPD: cnpd.public.lu).

Children

Exolander is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Retention

We keep purchase records as long as required for accounting and tax law. Data collected by Google, Gumroad and GitHub is kept according to their policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and publish the new version here.
